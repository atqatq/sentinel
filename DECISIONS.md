| D-018 | 2026-08-30 | H10 lands as a pure inbound-hardening gate (`hardening.js`) — the single choke point for every artifact: dropzone, watched-folder and email-in all ride `gateInboundFile`; the declared `source` is recorded but **never consulted** (proven by test), so email-in cannot have a softer path. Content is identified by **magic bytes, never by name**; the declared extension must agree with the content class (`DECLARED_MISMATCH` otherwise). The **XML family is refused outright** (`XML_REJECTED`) — XXE is made structurally impossible, not mitigated; if XML ever becomes a real input it lands behind a DTD-disabled parser with its own amendment. A ZIP must be an **XLSX workbook** (central directory carries `[Content_Types].xml` / `xl/*`) and passes **zip-bomb caps** before anything is inflated: entry count, inflated total, per-entry compression ratio above a 10 MiB floor (the floor protects small dense real XLSX parts), nested archives refused, ZIP64 refused at the boundary — caps are read from the central directory only, nothing is extracted. Formula injection is neutralized at the cell level with the **OWASP apostrophe escape** (`= + @ TAB CR`, and `-` only when not a plain canonical number); a neutralized cell that claimed a numeric column then fails the strict parser — hardening kills the execution vector, parse.js refuses the data. The **AV hook is fail-closed in every mode**: infected (`AV_INFECTED`), thrown (`AV_SCAN_ERROR`), malformed verdict (`AV_SCAN_ERROR`), and no-scanner-while-required (`AV_UNAVAILABLE`) — `avRequired` defaults to true, so production configures a scanner or the boundary refuses.
