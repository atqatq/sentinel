W15Module Management — the plugin control plane

Sentinel is fully modular and plugin-like: a small protected core (ledger, RBAC/RLS, ingestion boundary, module registry, audit) hosts every capability as an independent module with a manifest, its own failure domain, health probes and a circuit breaker. 02 · PLANE · PLAN

The engine is the verified heart of Sentinel: pure functions, decoded cell-by-cell from the Riyadh DDS workbook and re-verified byte-identical against its source spreadsheet. It is guarded by golden tests and must never be re-derived from first principles. All quantities are computed per recipe reference, per tenant, using working-day constants WD = 22 and WK = 4. The cards below walk the formulas in the order the engine itself evaluates them: consumption first, then the four planning parameters, then the derived stock levels, then the signals that drive proposals and status.

MRP

ENG-01

Material Requirements Planning

WHAT
The discipline of deciding what to order, how much, and by when so that production and sales never run out. Sentinel's MRP is a periodic-review policy: check stock against a reorder level every cycle, and order up to a target.

The whole PLAN plane; mathematically inside engine.ts / packages/core.
WHEN
Recomputed on every ingestion and on every parameter edit — results are visible instantly.
HOW
For each ref it derives daily consumption from deliveries, computes safety stock, reorder level and cycle order quantity, compares available stock against them, and emits a status plus, when below reorder, an order quantity.

04 · PLANE · PLAN

Demand enters Sentinel through exactly one primitive: deliveries — how many orders the business fulfilled per day, sourced from deliveries dashboard. Everything the engine believes about “how fast do we consume stock?” is derived from that number. Get deliveries wrong and every status, proposal and target distorts with it; that is why the deliveries guard (a ±50% variance check) is the most protective validation in the pipeline. This section covers the primitive, its normalization, and the seasonality overlay that may multiply it.

Deliveries

the raw demand primitive

WHAT
Count of customer deliveries per period — the only demand input Sentinel accepts. Riyadh's historical total is 737,750; the current driver is about 12,000 per day.
WHERE
Data Upload (template tab 6 6_DELIVERIES); Demand & Forecast screen (entry + history).
WHEN
Daily preferred; weekly, monthly, quarterly or YTD are accepted and normalized.
HOW
Accepted at any granularity and converted to a per-day rate by normalizeDeliveries() — invalid or negative input is rejected outright, never silently zeroed into a plan.

13 · PROOF · MEMORY

Every action and transaction appends a block to a hash-chained, append-only ledger. Any alteration breaks the chain and is detectable even by Origin — that property is what makes the audit trustworthy, and it is why the ledger is the last section before the platform itself. On top of the chain sit two user-facing superpowers: the time machine (render the whole system as of any date) and the date-vs-date diff (show exactly what changed and why).

LED-01

WHAT
The single ordered sequence every event appends to — business writes, access, authentication, system actions and denials all live in one chain, distinguished by class, so ordering and tamper-evidence are uniform.
WHERE

WHEN
Every event, always — and if a ledger write fails, the business transaction rolls back: an unlogged change must not be possible.
HOW
Retention is for the life of the system; no class is prunable ahead of the others (pruning would break the chain).
